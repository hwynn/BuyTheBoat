Settled form designs — layout, content, and sizing only

Every mockup below is something we've actually decided, pulled out of the much longer exploration files (form-architecture-layout-mockups.html, earmark-form-layout-mockups.html) so "what's in here" reliably means "what we chose," not "every idea we tried." Shown across several plan-health states so the content rules (planning/22 §6) are visible in practice, not just described.

Layout, positioning, content, and relative sizing only — not the visual design. Rounded corners, colored region borders/backgrounds: none of that was ever a style proposal, only a way to tell regions apart while planning where they go (see [[feedback-mockup-visual-style-scope]]). The real implementation is WPF/XAML and will match the aesthetic of the app's own first implementation, not this HTML. Read every box below for its position, its content, and how big it is relative to its neighbors — not its color.
